Recent Tabs — Help

Recent Tabs remembers the tabs you've used most recently in each Chrome window and lets you jump straight back to them.

Using the toolbar popup

Click the extension's icon in the toolbar to see your recently used tabs for the current window. Click any entry in the list to switch to it.

Cycling with the keyboard shortcut

Press Ctrl+Shift+Y (Cmd+Shift+Y on Mac) to open a small overlay showing your recently used tabs, with the next one highlighted. Keep pressing the shortcut to step further back through the list. The overlay closes automatically after about 1.5 seconds and switches to whichever tab is highlighted — or click an entry directly, or click elsewhere / press Escape to switch immediately.

If the shortcut doesn't do anything, it may not be assigned. Go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts, find "Recent Tabs", and set a key combination for "Cycle back through recently used tabs in this window".

Changing how many tabs are remembered

Click "Settings" at the bottom of the popup, or right-click the extension's toolbar icon and choose "Options". Choose 5, 10, 15, or 20 tabs remembered per window. This setting syncs across your Chrome installs if you're signed in.

What gets remembered, and for how long

  • Each window keeps its own separate list — switching tabs in one window doesn't affect another window's list.
  • The list resets when Chrome fully restarts. It does not persist across browser restarts by design.
  • Your "tabs remembered" setting, unlike the list itself, does persist and syncs via your Chrome account.

Privacy

Recent Tabs doesn't collect or transmit any data — everything stays local to your browser. See the full privacy policy for details.
