Recent Tabs — Privacy Policy

Recent Tabs does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any user data with any third party or server. There is no backend, no analytics, and the extension makes no network requests of any kind.

What the extension reads

Recent Tabs uses Chrome's tabs permission to read tab titles, favicon URLs, and tab/window IDs. This information is used solely to build and display the recently-used-tabs list within the extension's own popup, and to switch between tabs. This data never leaves your browser.

What the extension stores

The extension stores two things, both using Chrome's built-in storage APIs, both entirely local to your own browser/account:

  • Per-window recent-tabs lists — stored in chrome.storage.session, cleared automatically when Chrome fully restarts.
  • Your chosen "tabs remembered" setting — stored in chrome.storage.sync, which Chrome syncs across your own signed-in devices using your Google account's built-in sync. This data is not accessible to the developer of this extension or to anyone else.

Accounts and personal information

Recent Tabs does not require or collect any account, sign-in, or personal information.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the extension's listed support contact on the Chrome Web Store.
